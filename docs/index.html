License Overview & Philosophy

The license combines expansive usage freedom (including commercial exploitation, modification, redistribution, and sublicensing) with minimal but enforceable continuity conditions focused on notices, pass-through licensing, and non-endorsement.

Permissions, Conditions & Limitations

Core rights are granted in Sections 2 and 3. Conditions in Section 4 prevent downstream restrictions and misleading endorsement, while Sections 6–7 transfer warranty and liability risk away from licensor/contributors.

Legal Implications & Compliance

The agreement is structured around India-centric legal enforceability (law, venue, arbitration mechanics), automatic acceptance by conduct, patent-litigation termination triggers, and survival-oriented severability language.

Usage Guidelines & Best Practices

Distributors should preserve notices, keep license text accessible, avoid extra recipient restrictions, and maintain clear provenance and contribution records for both operational and legal reliability.
