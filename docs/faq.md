# LUCA – CLASSIC FAQ (Text-Grounded)

## 1. Can this license be used for commercial products?

Yes. Section 2 expressly permits use for any purpose, including commercial purposes.

## 2. Can I modify and redistribute derivatives?

Yes. Sections 2(b) and 2(c) allow it, provided Section 4 conditions are met.

## 3. Can I apply a stricter EULA to the LUCA-covered component?

Not if it adds restrictions on rights already granted under LUCA – CLASSIC (Section 4(b)).

## 4. Must I include full license text when distributing?

Yes—either include the full license copy or a canonical hyperlink as allowed by Section 4(b), and preserve notice requirements in Section 4(a).

## 5. Can I claim the original author endorses my fork?

No, unless explicit prior written permission exists (Section 4(c)(d)).

## 6. Is there a patent license?

Yes, from each contributor for necessarily infringed claims tied to their contributions (Section 3).

## 7. What happens if I file patent litigation over the work?

Patent rights under the license terminate as of filing date (Section 3 and Section 10.2).

## 8. Is this license automatically binding, or must it be registered?

The text states acceptance by conduct and no registration requirement (Section 8.4 and Section 16(a)).

## 9. Which law and forum apply?

Republic of India law applies; courts in New Delhi are designated, with arbitration option under Section 8.3.

## 10. Does this license transfer ownership of IP?

No. Section 9 preserves licensor ownership except express usage rights granted.

## 11. What if one clause is invalid in some jurisdiction?

Section 13 states invalid terms are reformed minimally and remaining terms continue.

## 12. Is there any warranty from licensor or contributors?

No. The work is provided "AS IS" with broad warranty and liability disclaimers (Section 6).
